# Sajha Kaam: the 5-minute presentation

One film, one voice, exactly 5:00. Each idea is **drawn** first (the hand-drawn story), then **proven** in
the **real app** (live recordings from sajha-kaam.onrender.com). The film plays, and you narrate over it:
record it as the film's voiceover, or read it live.

- **Pace:** about 2.3 words a second, calm. The script has about 400 words, about 3:20 spoken. For the
  other 1:40 the real app plays on its own, so the judges can watch the taps and screens.
- **Recording:** read straight through, and leave a **2-second pause at every `· · ·`**. I split the
  recording on those pauses and fit each scene to your voice.
- Times below are targets. The film follows your real timing.

## Running it live

1. **Open `Sajha-Kaam-Pitch.pptx` in PowerPoint** and choose Slide Show → **Use Presenter View** (Alt+F5
   on Windows). The audience sees only the film. You see the current beat's **SAY** lines, the **NEXT**
   beat, and a timer.
2. The slides play and move on by themselves. **Don't click.** Just speak. Each beat starts with an
   obvious change on screen (a new scene, or a chapter card in Act 2), so you can tell where you are.
3. If you fall behind, drop the second sentence of the beat. If you're ahead, let the app play: the
   real taps speak for themselves.
4. **Backup:** if PowerPoint misbehaves, play `pitch.mp4` full screen (it's the same film) and read
   from this page.
5. **Rehearse twice** with the film and a timer, out loud. The script fits with about 1:40 of breathing room.

## If you present live, keep it in your head as three lines

1. **The gap:** skilled youth have no way in, so they leave. Clients abroad don't trust strangers.
2. **The answer:** trusted Nepali Leaders abroad bring the work and guide young Nepalis. Agreement first, payment held, guidance, first pay, public proof.
3. **Why it lasts:** 15% only on accepted work, growth through trust, fair by design, and opportunity comes home.

## Likely judge questions (have one line ready)

- **"Why would a Leader do this?"** They earn the Leader's share of every job just for guiding and checking, without doing the work: 35% with a Starter, down to 5% with a Mentor, because a beginner needs more guidance.
- **"What stops a Leader keeping beginners cheap?"** Badges rise automatically by public rules. A Leader can only lower or pause a badge, with a reason the member sees, and takes at most 2 beginner jobs at once.
- **"Why would a client trust it?"** A written agreement, payment held until they accept, work checked by a verified Leader before they see it.
- **"Is payment real?"** The demo uses held payments without moving real money. eSewa, Khalti and bank withdrawals are shown as the flow.
