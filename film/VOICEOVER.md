# Sajha Kaam: voiceover scripts

Two recordings:

1. **Story**, about 3 minutes.
2. **Demo**, about 4½ minutes.

## How to record

- Use a quiet room and a phone held about 20 cm from your mouth. The voice memo app is fine.
- Read each script in one take.
- **Leave a 2-second pause at every `· · ·`**. The film uses these pauses to line your voice up with the scenes, so you don't need to watch the video while you read.
- Speak slowly and warmly, as if telling a friend a story. If you stumble, pause for 2 seconds and read the whole paragraph again. We'll keep the last try.
- Save the files as `story.m4a` and `demo.m4a` (mp3 or wav is fine too).

## Recording 1: Story (about 3:00)

*Answers Challenge 3: link Nepali talent with global clients and the diaspora, and bring opportunity home.*

**[1 · The gap]**
Nepal is full of skilled, ambitious young people. Sita builds websites in Pokhara. Bikash edits video in Kathmandu. But nothing connects them to clients abroad, and every job asks for experience they can't get. So every year, around two hundred thousand people leave.
· · ·

**[2 · The other side]**
In Melbourne, Emma runs Harbour Café. She needs a landing page and a promo video, but she doesn't trust strangers online. She trusts Rajan, a Nepali digital marketer. He has the clients and the know-how, but no time.
· · ·

**[3 · The solution]**
Sajha Kaam ties them together. Nepali professionals abroad become Team Leaders. They bring global work home, and young Nepalis do it, guided every step.
· · ·

**[4 · A Leader brings the client]**
Rajan writes the job and sends Emma one link on WhatsApp. She joins, and the job is already waiting.
· · ·

**[5 · Or the client posts it]**
Or a client posts the job in Sajha Kaam. Only verified Team Leaders can apply to lead it, and the client chooses.
· · ·

**[6 · The agreement]**
Before work starts, both sides agree: what's delivered, when it's finished, the deadline and the price. Emma pays, and the money is held until she accepts.
· · ·

**[7 · The team]**
Rajan taps Find people. Everyone with the skill sees the job and their own share. Sita and Bikash ask to join, and Rajan approves.
· · ·

**[8 · Guidance and feedback]**
In the job room, Rajan doesn't do the work. He guides it. He asks Sita for one change. She learns, and it gets better. Only then does it reach Emma.
· · ·

**[9 · First pay]**
Emma accepts, and the money is released: Sita and Bikash's first pay. Sajha Kaam keeps fifteen percent. The member's share grows with their badge, from fifty to eighty percent. Rajan earns the rest, for leading.
· · ·

**[10 · Proof]**
Each job becomes a verified entry on a public Work Record: the proof of experience Sita never had.
· · ·

**[11 · The journey]**
Members grow from Starter to Mentor, by public rules. A Leader can't hold anyone back for profit, and takes only two beginner jobs at a time.
· · ·

**[12 · From member to Leader]**
A Mentor can start her own team. Others apply with proof, and Sajha Kaam verifies them. Every new Leader brings more work home.
· · ·

**[13 · Opportunity comes home]**
Sita and Bikash didn't have to leave to work for the world. The work came home. Sajha Kaam: shared work. साझा काम।

*(About 350 spoken words, roughly 3 minutes at a calm pace with the pauses. The figure in beat 1 comes from the hackathon's own challenge statement.)*

## Recording 2: Demo (about 4:30)

**[Intro]**
Now let's see it working. This is the real app, running live. Four phones: Rajan, the Team Leader; Emma, the client; and Sita and Bikash, two new members.
· · ·

**[Chapter 1 · Rajan brings his client]**
Rajan writes the job for Emma's landing page: what will be delivered, when it counts as finished, the days and the budget. Emma isn't on Sajha Kaam yet, so the app makes an invite link, and Rajan sends it to her on WhatsApp.
· · ·
Emma taps the link and joins in seconds. The agreement is already waiting. She reads it, taps Agree, and pays. The payment is held until she accepts the work. She does the same for the promo video.
· · ·

**[Chapter 2 · Find people]**
Now Rajan needs people. He taps Find people, and everyone with that skill gets a notification. Sita sees the project and her own share, fifty percent as a new Starter, and asks to join. Bikash does the same for the video. Rajan approves them both.
· · ·

**[Chapter 3 · The work room]**
Each job opens a shared room. Sita says hello, and Rajan tells her Emma loves her latte art. Bikash uploads his promo video right here.
· · ·

**[Chapter 4 · Check and approve]**
They send their work for checking. The quick check ticks off the "finished when" list. Rajan asks Sita for one change, the latte art in the hero. She fixes it and sends it again. Rajan approves both and sends them to Emma.
· · ·

**[Chapter 5 · Accept and pay]**
Emma sees her new page. She taps Accept and release. The money is shared instantly: Sita, Rajan, and fifteen percent to Sajha Kaam.
· · ·

**[Chapter 6 · First pay]**
Sita's first pay. She sends it to eSewa. Bikash sends his to Khalti. And Rajan sees his share for checking the work.
· · ·

**[Chapter 7 · Work Record and badges]**
The job is now a verified entry on Sita's Work Record. On Rajan's team page, everyone can see the next badge and exactly what it needs. Badges grow by the rules, not by the Leader.
· · ·

**[Chapter 8 · When a client posts directly]**
Clients can also come on their own. Emma posts a new job herself: an opening-week reel. It goes to verified Team Leaders and Mentors with the video skill. Anusha, a verified Leader, applies. Pemba, a Mentor, applies with a short video about herself. Emma compares them and chooses. From here it's the same safe path: agreement, held payment, work room, check, accept.
· · ·

**[Chapter 9 · Leaders]**
Pemba is a five-star Mentor, so her record makes her a Team Leader with one tap. Others send proof of their work, and the Sajha Kaam reviewer checks it before they're verified.
· · ·

**[Close]**
That's Sajha Kaam. Leaders bring the clients, young Nepalis do the work and build a real record, and everyone is paid fairly. Shared work. साझा काम।

*(About 560 words, roughly 4½ minutes with the pauses.)*
