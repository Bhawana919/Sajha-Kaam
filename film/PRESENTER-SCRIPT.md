# Sajha Kaam: presenter script (5 minutes, Google Meet)

Read this on your phone, on paper or in a second window, **not** on the screen you share.
Every slide plays and moves on by itself. **Don't click. Just speak.**

- **CUE** is what appears on screen when a slide starts. When you see it, start that slide's lines.
- **SAY** is your words. They are timed to fit, with room to breathe.
- If you fall behind, drop the *italic* sentence. If you're ahead, pause and let the app play.

## ACT 2 · HOW IT WORKS: THE REAL APP

### Slide 5 · A Leader brings the client (0:45–1:15)
**CUE:** card **"1 · Leader brings the client"**, then Rajan's phone filling in a job.
**SAY:**
> Rajan writes the job: what will be delivered, when it's finished, the deadline and the price. He sends Emma one link. She joins, agrees, and pays, *and the money is held until she accepts the work.*

**NEXT:** Emma posts a job herself.

### Slide 6 · Or the client posts it (1:15–1:38)
**CUE:** card **"2 · Client posts"**, then the "verified only" gate.
**SAY:**
> Clients can also post a job themselves. Only verified Team Leaders, and top Mentors, can apply to lead it. *Emma compares them and chooses.*

**NEXT:** finding the team.

### Slide 7 · The Leader builds the team (1:38–2:02)
**CUE:** card **"3 · Team"**, then share cards on two phones.
**SAY:**
> Rajan taps Find people. Everyone with the skill sees the job and their own share. *Sita and Bikash ask to join, and Rajan approves them.*

**NEXT:** the job room.

### Slide 8 · Guided, not done for them (2:02–2:35)
**CUE:** card **"4 · Guided"**, then the job room.
**SAY:**
> In the job room they talk, share drafts and upload work. Rajan doesn't do the work, he guides it. He asks Sita for one change. *She fixes it, and only then does it go to Emma.*

**NEXT:** Emma accepts.

### Slide 9 · First pay (2:35–3:10)
**CUE:** card **"5 · First pay"**, then Emma's new café page.
**SAY:**
> Emma sees her new page and accepts. The money is released: Sajha Kaam keeps fifteen percent, the member gets fifty to eighty percent by badge, and Rajan earns the rest for leading. *Sita and Bikash just got their first pay.*

**NEXT:** the Work Record.

### Slide 10 · Proof, and the journey (3:10–3:32)
**CUE:** card **"6 · Proof"**, then the Work Record stamp.
**SAY:**
> Every job becomes a verified entry on a public Work Record: the experience Sita never had. *Badges grow by public rules, from Starter to Mentor, and no Leader can hold anyone back.*

**NEXT:** Pemba becomes a Leader.

### Slide 11 · From member to Leader (3:32–3:55)
**CUE:** card **"7 · Leaders"**, then Pemba's phone.
**SAY:**
> A Mentor can start her own team, because her record is the proof. *Anyone else applies with proof, and we verify them.*

## Questions: one line each

- **Why would a Leader do this?** They earn the Leader's share of every job just for guiding and checking, without doing the work: 35% with a Starter, down to 5% with a Mentor, because a beginner needs more guidance.
- **What stops a Leader keeping beginners cheap?** Badges rise automatically by public rules. A Leader can only lower a badge or pause one promotion, with a reason the member sees, and can take at most 2 beginner jobs at once.
- **Why would a client trust it?** A written agreement, payment held until they accept, and work checked by a verified Leader before they see it.
- **How do Leaders get verified?** A five-star Mentor's record verifies her at once. Anyone else sends proof of their work, and a Sajha Kaam reviewer approves or declines with a reason.
- **How does it make money?** 15% of every accepted job, and nothing when work isn't accepted.
- **Is the payment real?** In the demo, payments are held and released without moving real money. Withdrawals to eSewa, Khalti or a bank show the real flow.
- **Is this a real app?** Yes. Everything in Act 2 was recorded on the live site you can open now, sajha-kaam.onrender.com, and there's an Android app too.
