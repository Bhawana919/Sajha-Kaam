# Sajha Kaam: presenter script

**5 minutes · Google Meet · three speakers: Santoshi Gelal, Bhawana Ojha, Shova Gelal**

Read this on your phone or on paper, **not** on the screen you share. The film plays and moves from slide to
slide by itself. **Nobody clicks. You just speak when your cue appears.**

- **CUE** is what appears on screen when a slide starts. When you see it, start that slide's lines.
- **SAY** is the words, timed to fit with room to breathe.
- If you fall behind, drop the *italic* sentence. If you're ahead, pause and let the app play.
- **No hand-over sentences** ("Now Shova will…"). Each speaker starts the moment their cue appears.

## Questions: who answers

**Santoshi: business, money, market**
- **How does it make money?** 15% of every accepted job, and nothing when work isn't accepted, so our income follows real value delivered.
- **Why would a Leader do this?** They earn the Leader's share of every job just for guiding and checking, without doing the work: 35% with a Starter, down to 5% with a Mentor, because a beginner needs more guidance.
- **What stops a Leader keeping beginners cheap?** Badges rise automatically by public rules. A Leader can only lower a badge or pause one promotion, with a reason the member sees, and can take at most 2 beginner jobs at once.
- **Who are the first customers?** Nepali-run businesses abroad (cafés, shops, restaurants) reached through Leaders who already know them. Every Leader brings their own clients, so we don't need to buy them.
- **How does this answer the challenge?** It links Nepali talent with global clients through the diaspora, and young people earn, learn and build proof without leaving.

**Bhawana: product and trust**
- **Why would a client trust it?** A written agreement, payment held until they accept, and work checked by a verified Leader before they see it.
- **How do Leaders get verified?** A five-star Mentor's record verifies her at once. Anyone else sends proof of their work, and a Sajha Kaam reviewer approves or declines with a reason.
- **What happens when the client wants changes?** Changes to an agreed job need both sides to agree. In the work room, the Leader asks for changes before the client ever sees the work.

**Shova: technology and the build**
- **Is this a real app?** Yes. Everything in the demo was recorded on the live site, sajha-kaam.onrender.com, and there's an Android app too.
- **Is the payment real?** In the demo, payments are held and released without moving real money. Withdrawals to eSewa, Khalti or a bank show the real flow, and a payment provider plugs in where the demo holds the money.
- **How is the Work Record verified?** Each finished job is digitally signed when the client accepts it, so anyone can check that the entry is genuine.
- **What's it built with?** One codebase for the Android and web app (Expo, React Native), a Python FastAPI server with a SQL database, Cloudinary for photo and video uploads, and hosting on Render.

## A 4-hour practice plan

1. **Read-through (20 min).** Each speaker reads their own slides aloud. Mark any word you trip on, and
   swap it for an easier one if needed. Keep the meaning the same.
2. **Run with the film, together (3 runs, about 30 min).** Play `pitch-with-link.mp4`, everyone speaks on
   their cues. On run 1, notice where you're late. By run 3, nobody should need the italic sentences.
3. **Hand-over drill (10 min).** Practise only the switches: slide 3 → 4 (Santoshi → Bhawana),
   8 → 9 (Bhawana → Shova), 11 → 12 (Shova → Santoshi), 13 → 14 (Santoshi → Bhawana). Start on the cue, with no pause.
4. **Questions round (20 min).** One person asks the questions above in random order; the right person answers in one or two sentences.
5. **Full dress rehearsal in Google Meet (15 min).** Exactly as on the day: one person shares the Chrome tab,
   everyone unmuted, headphones on. Time it.
6. **Rest.** Wake the server 15 minutes before, and ask Claude to reset the live demo data shortly before you present.
